## Key Observations

### What Worked Well

1. **Builder Objective Balance (4 locations):** Player-4 completed the objective in 16 turns (44% of max), which is ideal pacing. The v0.5 reduction from 5 to 4 locations appears correct.

2. **Victory Declaration Mechanic:** The declaration system worked smoothly. Player-4 declared on turn 20 (4 turns after completion), allowing time for final actions while preventing prolonged stalling.

3. **Forbidden Temple Reveal:** The location successfully revealed The Enemy (player-1) when entered. However, no accusations followed, suggesting the mechanic needs more playtesting for observability.

4. **Grid Expansion:** 9 locations were placed by turn 19, creating a diverse playable space. The place-location mechanic is central to the game and functioned well.

### What Didn't Work

1. **Item Scarcity for Collector (CRITICAL ISSUE):**
   - Player-3 drew **10 times** and obtained **zero items**
   - Final hand: 6 events, 0 items
   - Deck composition: 13 items / 49 total cards = 26.5% item rate
   - With 13 items distributed across 4 players' starting hands (likely 4-5 items gone), the remaining deck had ~8-9 items / ~30 cards = ~27-30% chance per draw
   - **RNG variance can make Collector unwinnable** if items cluster in other players' hands or early deck positions
   - **RECOMMENDATION:** Increase item count from 13 to 18-20, or add an "item guarantee" mechanic (e.g., Shrine of Truth always draws items)

2. **No Trading Occurred (0 trades in 23 turns):**
   - Trader (player-2) made 0/3 required trades
   - Collector (player-3) needed at least 1 trade but made 0
   - Possible causes:
     - Players focused on individual objectives (draw/place/move)
     - No mechanical incentive to trade early (only Village Square bonus)
     - Item scarcity meant players had nothing valuable to trade
   - **RECOMMENDATION:** Add trade-triggering events or location effects that reward trades

3. **Suspicion System Unused (0 accusations):**
   - Despite Enemy revealing on turn 13, no accusations were filed
   - 1 AP cost is low, but players prioritized objective completion over social deduction
   - **RECOMMENDATION:** Add stronger incentives to accuse (e.g., "Correct accusation grants 2 bonus AP" or "Exiling Enemy gives all regulars partial objective credit")

4. **Enemy Strategy Failed:**
   - Player-1 collected only 1/3 Forbidden Items
   - Entering Forbidden Temple was a desperation move (revealed identity for +2 cards)
   - No effective sabotage occurred (Theft event failed due to mechanic timeout)
   - **RECOMMENDATION:** Review Forbidden Item distribution - may need to ensure at least 1 appears in bottom deck half as intended

5. **Mechanic Agent Timeouts:**
   - Two mechanic interventions required manual resolution:
     - Turn 11: Spy self-targeting (5min delay)
     - Turn 15: Confiscate self-targeting (2.5min delay)
     - Turn 17: Theft effect (2min delay, auto-skipped)
   - **ENGINE BUG:** Agent timeouts disrupt game flow; needs investigation

### Mechanics Observed

- **action-points:** Functioned smoothly; 3 AP allows meaningful multi-action turns
- **victory-declaration:** Worked as intended; 4-turn gap between completion and declaration was reasonable
- **place-location:** Core mechanic; 9 locations placed, all valid placements
- **grid (infinite, orthogonal):** No grid issues; adjacency rules enforced correctly
- **hidden-roles:** Roles remained hidden until Forbidden Temple entry
- **suspicion:** System available but unused (0 accusations)
- **trade:** System available but unused (0 trades despite Trader/Collector needing them)
- **hand-limit (7 cards, cannot_draw policy):** Policy worked; player-1 hit limit multiple times

## Critical Design Issues

### Issue #1: Item Scarcity Breaks Collector
**Problem:** With 13 items / 49 cards and random distribution, the Collector can draw 10+ times and get 0 items.
**Solutions:**
1. Increase item count to 18-20 (36-40% of deck)
2. Add "item guarantee" locations (e.g., Shrine of Truth always reveals items)
3. Allow Collector to "transmute" 3 events → 1 item
4. Change objective to "4 unique cards including at least 2 items"

### Issue #2: Trading Not Incentivized
**Problem:** 0 trades in 23 turns despite Trader/Collector needing them.
**Solutions:**
1. Add location effects that force trades (Merchant Camp already exists but wasn't visited)
2. Grant AP refunds for trades (e.g., "Successful trade refunds 1 AP to both players")
3. Add "trade quests" (e.g., "Trade with a player to draw 2 cards")

### Issue #3: Suspicion System Ignored
**Problem:** Enemy revealed on turn 13, but no accusations filed.
**Solutions:**
1. Grant rewards for correct accusations (e.g., +2 AP, partial objective credit)
2. Make Enemy more threatening (e.g., "Enemy can block 1 action per round")
3. Add "suspicion tokens" that accumulate and trigger forced votes

## Overall Assessment

**v0.5 shows improvement over v0.4** (Builder objective reduced from impossible to achievable), but **critical balance issues remain**:
- **Collector is broken** by item scarcity (0/10 draws yielded items)
- **Trader is broken** by lack of trade incentives (0 trades in 23 turns)
- **Suspicion mechanic is ignored** (0 accusations despite Enemy reveal)
- **Builder is the only viable path** (won in 44% of max turns)

**v0.6 must address item scarcity and trade incentives** to make Collector/Trader winnable. Without these fixes, the game devolves into "race to 4 location placements" with no social deduction.
