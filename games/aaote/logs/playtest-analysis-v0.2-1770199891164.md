## Critical Discovery: Objective Recognition Problem

**Major concern:** Multiple players likely completed their objectives but didn't declare victory.

**Evidence:**
- player-1, player-2, player-4 all visited 6+ locations (Explorer requirement)
- None declared victory
- All passed repeatedly in late game

**Possible explanations:**
1. None of them were the Explorer (unlikely with 3/5 players reaching 6 locations)
2. Players didn't know the victory declaration mechanic
3. Players miscounted their progress
4. Players thought they needed to complete all objectives (misunderstanding)

**Recommendation:** Add automated victory checking or explicit objective progress display.

## Recommendations for v0.3

### Priority 1: Victory Condition Clarity
1. **Add objective progress tracking** to player state display
   - "Explorer: 6/6 locations visited — YOU CAN DECLARE VICTORY"
   - "Trader: 1/4 trades completed"
   - "Builder: 2/5 locations placed"
2. **Automatic victory check** option (verify conditions, prompt declaration)
3. **Tutorial prompt** when victory is achievable

### Priority 2: Endgame Pacing
1. **Reduce max turns to 30-35** (turns 41-49 were non-interactive)
2. **Add pressure mechanic** after turn 30:
   - "Suspicion rises" — players must vote on Enemy identity
   - Failed votes eliminate accusers
   - Creates endgame tension
3. **Enemy reveal at turn 35** if not already revealed (force confrontation)

### Priority 3: Trading Incentives
1. **Reduce Trader requirement to 3 trades**
2. **Add "trade-locked" items:**
   - Certain items start in specific players' hands
   - Can only be obtained via trade
   - Forces interaction for Collector/Explorer
3. **Village Square bonus:** Trades here give both players +1 card draw

### Priority 4: Builder Viability
1. **Reduce Builder requirement to 3-4 locations**
2. **Add placement bonuses:**
   - "First to place 3 locations: Draw 2 cards"
   - Makes rushing placement viable
3. **Limit grid size** (max 12 locations) to create scarcity

### Priority 5: Forbidden Item Awareness
1. **Mark Forbidden Items visually** (red border, skull icon)
2. **Warning prompt** when trading a Forbidden Item:
   - "This is a Forbidden Item. Trading it may help The Enemy win."
3. **Add "Appraisal" action** (1 AP): Identify if an item is Forbidden

## Conclusion

AAOTE v0.2 achieved a significant milestone: the first observed Enemy victory via timeout with all 3 Forbidden Items. Player-5's stealth strategy (accepting a gifted Cursed Amulet, holding the other Forbidden Items, then passing to run out the clock) demonstrates that The Enemy can win through patience and defensive play.

**Critical findings:**
1. **Enemy balance appears correct** — Victory is achievable but requires item collection and survival
2. **Objective recognition is broken** — Multiple players likely completed objectives but didn't declare victory
3. **Endgame needs pacing fixes** — Turns 41-49 were non-interactive (all passes)
4. **Trading needs incentives** — Only 1/6 trade offers accepted
5. **Forbidden Items need visual clarity** — player-2 gifted Cursed Amulet, possibly unknowingly

**Critical fixes for v0.3:**
1. Add objective progress tracking with victory prompts
2. Reduce max turns to 30-35 to eliminate stagnation
3. Mark Forbidden Items visually to prevent accidental gifting
4. Reduce Trader requirement to 3 trades
5. Reduce Builder requirement to 3-4 locations

**Positive takeaways:**
- Engine handled 49 turns flawlessly with 5 players
- Timeout victory mechanic works correctly
- Forbidden Item trading creates high-stakes decisions
- Enemy stealth strategy is viable and balanced
- Grid exploration reached 6+ locations for multiple players

**Biggest surprise:** Player-2 gifting the Cursed Amulet to The Enemy on turn 7 directly enabled the victory. This could be either a brilliant deception attempt (frame player-5) or a critical mistake (didn't recognize Forbidden Items).

The game demonstrates strong core mechanics but needs clarity improvements and endgame pacing fixes before the next playtest.
