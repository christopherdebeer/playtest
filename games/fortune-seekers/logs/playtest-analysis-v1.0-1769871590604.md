# Fortune Seekers - Game Analysis

## Summary

A 22-turn push-your-luck drafting game where **player-1** won with 40 points after the max turn limit was reached. Both players employed conservative strategies, banking dice rolls early and focusing on card drafting over risk-taking. The game showcased the tension between safe play and the push-your-luck mechanic.

## Winner

**player-1** - Score: 40 points

## Win Condition

Game ended at **max turns (20)** without either player reaching the 100-point target. Player-1 won by having the higher score (40 vs 30).

## Mechanics Observed

- **Push Your Luck**: Dice rolling with bust risk - players consistently chose to bank after single successful rolls (6 roll events, 6 bank events)
- **Open Drafting**: Card selection from visible display worked smoothly - 14 cards drafted throughout game
- **Variable Player Powers**:
  - player-1: "Lucky" (never bust on 1) - power unused due to conservative play
  - player-2: "Cautious" (can bank anytime) - aligned with their strategy

## Player Strategies

### player-1 (40 points - Winner)
- Focused on high-value treasure cards (3 Diamonds played = 60 pts)
- Conservative dice rolling - always banked after first success
- "Lucky" power was wasted due to never pushing luck far enough to risk bust
- Final scoring: ~70 pts from cards, 30 pts from dice

### player-2 (30 points)
- Drafted Crown Jewel early for quick 30-point boost
- Matched conservative dice strategy
- "Cautious" power complemented safe play style
- Lower card values selected overall

## Recommendations

1. **Scoring threshold**: 100 points may be too high for 20 turns - consider reducing to 75 or increasing max turns
2. **Push incentives**: Players never pushed their luck beyond 1 roll - consider bonus for consecutive successful rolls
3. **Variable power impact**: Powers didn't significantly affect gameplay due to conservative meta - consider more impactful abilities
4. **Draft display**: All 5 cards were consistently treasure/modifiers - risk cards (Gambler's Ruin) rarely drafted
